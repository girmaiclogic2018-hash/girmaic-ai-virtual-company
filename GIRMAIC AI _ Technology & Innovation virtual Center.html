Zero-budget launch path

Build the company in phases.

Free tools help us start, but payments, AI usage, custom domains, and higher traffic may eventually involve costs or eligibility restrictions.

PHASE 01 · NOW

Establish the foundation

Publish a static website, check links, and keep legal registration separate.

PHASE 02 · VALIDATE

Choose one offer

Test one digital product or service. Confirm pricing, payment, refund terms, and delivery.

PHASE 03 · AUTOMATE

Connect the workflow

Use Make.com for notifications only after the manual process works. Verify payments server-side.

PHASE 04 · GROW

Prove and expand

Collect feedback, improve security, document impact, and pursue suitable grants.

Important: A website does not register a company. Confirm Ethiopian name approval, legal structure, address, tax, licensing, and outside-employment requirements with relevant authorities before operating as a registered company.
